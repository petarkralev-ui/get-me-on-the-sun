Income

We help set the property up to earn

📋

We handle the rental licence application first

Before a property can be rented properly, the licence position needs to be checked and the right application handled. This is not always a simple process, but we can coordinate the paperwork, local requirements, and follow-up so the rental plan starts on the right footing.

🏝️

We help assess the right rental route before you advertise

Tenerife can be a strong place to earn income from a property, but the rental route must be checked before it is marketed. If tourist rental use is not available or not suitable for that property, a longer rental route, often from one month or more, can still be a practical way to earn money when you are not using the apartment yourself or when the property is bought as an investment.

Official rental status check

We check the legal route before the income plan

The rules can change, so we check the current official position before advising whether the property should be used for tourist rental, longer rental, or private family use between owner visits.

Check before marketing

EU buyers: the property checks are broadly the same, but tax residency and personal reporting should be reviewed before income starts.

UK buyers: the property checks are broadly the same, with extra attention to non-EU stay planning, the 90/180-day Schengen rule, and non-resident tax advice.

We arrange local management around the property

Handling bookings, cleaning, check-ins, and guest issues remotely is hard to do well from abroad. We help arrange the local management, cleaning, guest communication, and handover routines that make renting realistic when you are not living on the island full-time.

Protection

Keeping small problems small

🔧

We keep local eyes on the property

The single biggest risk for an owner who is not on the island full-time is a small maintenance issue going unnoticed until it becomes an expensive one. We help put trusted local contacts in place who can check the property, respond quickly, coordinate repairs, and protect its value between your visits.
