Canary Islands rules first

ZEC is a Canary Islands regime within the Spanish tax system. We apply the Canary Islands REF, ZEC, RIC, and IGIC rules where they govern the business. Spanish national company, corporate-tax, treaty, and non-resident rules still apply where the Canary Islands regime does not replace them.

Canary Islands Regime

We check what genuinely fits your business

💼

ZEC can be powerful, but the 4% rate is conditional

A company or branch admitted to the Canary Islands Special Zone (ZEC) may pay 4% Corporate Income Tax on qualifying operations carried out materially and effectively in the Canary Islands. The rate applies only within statutory taxable-base and state-aid limits; other income can be taxed under the rate otherwise applicable to the company. We first check the activity, local substance, investment, employment, and business plan, then coordinate specialist approval and setup.

🌍

International businesses need joined-up planning

Dividends, interest, capital gains, rental income, and ownership structure can interact with your home country's rules. Tax treaties and EU rules may help, but they do not produce the same result for every owner or country. We coordinate Spanish and home-country advice before the structure is fixed.

Qualifying

The main ZEC conditions in Tenerife

ZEC is a regulated business regime, not an automatic benefit of buying a property or registering a company. For a Tenerife application, the plan will normally need to address all of the following:

01

Company and local management

A new company or branch must be entered in the ZEC register, with its registered office and effective management in the Canary Islands. At least one director or legal representative must live in the Canary Islands.

02

Business investment

In Tenerife, the standard minimum is EUR 100,000 in qualifying fixed assets within the first two years. The investment must support the approved business activity; special exceptions require ZEC authorization.

03

Local employment

The standard Tenerife requirement is at least five jobs created within six months of authorization and maintained while the company remains in ZEC. The business plan must show that the operation is real and viable.

04

Approved activity

The activity must appear in the statutory ZEC list. Telecommunications and computer programming, consultancy, and related IT activities are included, but the precise service and where it is performed still need to be checked.

Company Setup

We coordinate the process from foreign parent to Spanish subsidiary

A Spanish S.L. subsidiary is a separate Spanish company owned wholly or partly by a foreign parent. It acquires its legal personality when it is registered at the Commercial Registry. The exact route varies, but a typical managed process includes:

01

Choose the structure

Confirm whether a subsidiary, branch, or another structure fits the activity, ownership, liability, tax position, and ZEC plan before documents are prepared.

02

Prepare foreign documents

Obtain evidence that the parent company exists, its constitutional documents, ownership information, resolutions, and powers of attorney. Documents may require an apostille or legalisation and a sworn Spanish translation.

03

Complete Spanish formalities

Arrange the required Spanish tax identification numbers, company-name certificate, incorporation deed, notary appointment, share capital, and Commercial Registry filing.

04

Activate and maintain the company

Complete the final NIF and tax census registration, banking and anti-money-laundering checks, Social Security registration where staff are hired, foreign-investment declarations, accounting, and ongoing filings.

Route 1 — EU Companies

A practical EU route for growing into Tenerife

An EU company can own a Spanish subsidiary and, where the legal conditions are met, may use EU restructuring rules to bring an existing Canary Islands company into the group without a conventional cash purchase. Czechia is a useful example of how this can work.

1

Exchange shares instead of funding a cash purchase

The owners of the Canary Islands S.L. transfer their shares to the EU holding company and receive shares in the holding company. This can preserve cash for staff, equipment, premises, and business growth.

2

Build a clear commercial case

A professional valuation, a fair exchange ratio, majority voting control, company approvals, and genuine business reasons are essential. When every legal condition is met, Spanish tax on the exchange may be deferred rather than charged immediately.

3

We coordinate the Spanish process

We help organise Spanish tax numbers, translated and legalised company documents, resolutions, powers of attorney, the formal signing, Tax Agency communication, and foreign-investment declaration.

4

Protect the structure before signing

Tax deferral is not automatic. We arrange the necessary professional checks, including the special transfer-tax risk where a company mainly holds Spanish property that is not used in a genuine business activity.

Route 2 — UK Companies

Tenerife remains open to UK business

Brexit changed the tax route, but it did not prevent a UK company from owning and operating a Spanish subsidiary. We help UK businesses establish the right Spanish structure and coordinate the extra cross-border checks from the start.

1

A UK parent can own a Spanish S.L.

The Spanish company can trade, employ staff, hold business assets, and apply for ZEC status. ZEC approval depends on the Spanish company's qualifying activity, management, investment, employment, and real presence in the Canary Islands, not simply on the nationality of its owner.

2

The EU share-exchange route is different after Brexit

A UK acquiring company does not fall within the EU restructuring directive used in the Czech example. The same Spanish tax deferral should not be assumed. We ask Spanish and UK tax advisers to approve the structure before any shares are transferred.

3

UK tax relief may still be available

UK share-exchange rules may defer a UK capital gain when their conditions are met. HMRC advance clearance is optional but can provide valuable certainty and must be obtained before the new shares are issued.

4

Treaty and investment checks can protect the plan

The UK-Spain treaty can provide favourable dividend treatment, including a possible Spanish withholding-tax exemption for a qualifying UK company with at least 10% ownership. Beneficial ownership, anti-abuse rules, and any Spanish foreign-investment approval must be checked first.

Other Incentives

ZEC is only one part of the Canary Islands system

IGIC

Canary Islands indirect tax

The general IGIC rate is 7%, rather than mainland Spanish VAT. ZEC rules can exempt imports by ZEC entities and certain supplies between ZEC entities, but not every purchase or sale is automatically exempt.

RIC

Reinvestment of Canary profits

The RIC may reduce the taxable base by up to 90% of qualifying undistributed profit from Canary establishments when the reserve is recorded and invested within the legal deadline in permitted assets or activities. The rules for eligible investment and maintenance are detailed.

R&D

Technology deductions

Canary Islands R&D and technological-innovation deductions can be valuable, but ordinary software development, maintenance, or minor updates do not automatically qualify. The project and expenditure need technical and tax review.

Limits

No automatic stacking

Incentives may interact, but they are not fully interchangeable. RIC cannot be combined with specified deductions for the same assets and costs, and EU state-aid limits can cap the total benefit.

Important — this is why we coordinate advice

Buying or holding a property in Tenerife does not, by itself, qualify for ZEC. A property activity should never be presented as eligible until its exact classification appears in the permitted-activity list and the ZEC authorities approve the business plan.

The RIC also has strict property rules. In particular, investment in a holiday-rental home is excluded. A special route for long-term residential rental has applied to qualifying investments since 2025, but it carries detailed conditions and is not a general tax break for any rented apartment.

Our role is to coordinate the process and the qualified professionals around your particular facts. Tax deferral, exemptions, reduced rates, treaty relief, and ZEC authorization are never guaranteed.
